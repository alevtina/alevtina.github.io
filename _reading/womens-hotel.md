Set in a fictional 1960s New York women's hotel loosely modeled on the real Barbizon, the novel follows a cast including Katherine the first-floor manager, Lucianne, Kitty, Ruth, Pauline, and Stephen the elevator operator. It's been pitched as being in the vein of Dawn Powell and Rona Jaffe, with the immersive charm of *The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel* and *Lessons in Chemistry*. For me, it didn't come close to living up to that comparison. 

I couldn't get into it. The characters felt like stock types rather than people, the humor didn't land, and the whole thing read as a string of anecdotes rather than a story with real momentum. I never connected with it and didn't finish.
