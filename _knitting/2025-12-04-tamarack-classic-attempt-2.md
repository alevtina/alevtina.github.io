**2026-11-17**: Worker to listed length, ending on a round that ends in K,P before GSS.

**2026-11-16**: Increased on the 1st round of 8-round Moss Stitch pattern repeats a total of 4 times, a total of 32 rounds. (I have 49 sts on the needles.) I need another 14 sts, with increases every 6th row. I’ll have to figure out a cadence that I’m comfortable with that I can repeat on the other sleeve when it comes time.
*Edited to add*: I’ll increase on the 1st round of 4-round Moss Stitch pattern repeats. I did one repeat, now up to 51 sts.

**2026-11-15**: Picking this back up! Used what I think is the Italian tubular cast-on method to start the first sleeve. (I looked up videos of the tubular cast-on method. The one by Andrea Mowry did not do the waste yarn cast-on and increase row like the pattern wanted. It seemed easier than the pattern’s explanation so I went with it!) I made the cuff and got a few inches of the arm done before I realized I used US 10.5 needles (not the US 10.75 needles like I was supposed to!) after the cuff :melting_face: So I shoved a circular needle through where I thought the cuff ended and unraveled. (I spent way too much time finding the right needle size not to use it!) Got pretty close and only had to clean up after myself a little bit. I re-knit but didn’t get as far up the arm as I did earlier before getting too sleepy to continue. Stopped just before the second increase round.

**2025-12-16**: Hallelujah! With US10.75 (7mm) needles, my gauge is 14 sts x 24 rows = 4”. Stitch gauge is right on but row gauge is a tad more compressed. I can live with that! I will proceed with my selected size and knit to pattern specs.

**2025-12-15**: New needle tips came in today so I made a third pocket. Will check gauge when “swatch” is dry :crossed_fingers::skin-tone-3:

**2025-12-07**: With US 11 needles, gauge was too loose at 13 sts and 20 rows = 4”. Ordered US 10.75 needle tips from KnitPicks and hope that’s my Goldilocks!

**2025-12-05**: Used US 10.5 needles and got 15 sts x 24 rows = 4”. That’s too tight :\ Trying with US 11 needles but fear it’ll be too loose!

**2025-12-04**: Knit one patch pocket using US 10.5 needles. I’m blocking it now and will measure gauge.
